How this test works
  • Exact identifiers only, never names. Routes, in the order the best mix takes its first hit: Brandfetch ISIN, Brandfetch ticker, logo.dev ticker, Brandfetch domain, logo.dev domain. A company with no hit would get a coloured letter mark in the app.
  • Ticker: KOSPI 005930.KS, KOSDAQ 247540.KQ, KONEX .KS (logo.dev has no KONEX suffix); US as listed (BRK-B). A bare 6-digit code is never tried: Chinese exchanges use 6-digit codes too, so it is not exact. ISIN: from the KRX listing (check digit verified), else the exact common-share rule; none for US. Domain: the company's own homepage as filed with DART, Korea only; platform hosts (blog.naver.com, facebook.com and the like) and a domain that two companies share are not used.
  • No logo = image error. Every request asks for HTTP 404 when there is no logo (Brandfetch fallback/404, logo.dev fallback=404), so a miss never shows a placeholder. A browser cannot read the status of another site's image, so: logo.dev answers a domain it has never seen with a placeholder image (HTTP 202) on the first request; every logo.dev domain hit is therefore loaded again after 5½ minutes ("re" badge) and only the second answer counts.
  • Checks: before the test, each provider must show a known logo (Apple) and must give no image for made-up identifiers; during the test a known logo is checked again every 100 loads and a made-up ticker every 300. If a known logo stops loading (rate limit, block, ad blocker, network), the provider pauses and its recent misses are tried again; after three failed checks that provider stops and its untested routes show "off" (not counted as misses). A route that answers a made-up identifier with an image is switched off and not counted. Checks run one at a time per provider, beside the route queues.
  • Best mix counts a company once all its routes have finished. A company whose routes were all "off" or timed out is "not tested" and left out; one with no logo where some route did not run counts as no logo (shown in the first-hit line).
  • Pace: at most 2 requests a second to Brandfetch and 4 a second to logo.dev, one at a time per route.
  • Sample (fixed seed, listed below, all from the data date shown): KR-A = the 200 largest Korean companies by market cap (all of them); KR-B to KR-E = 300 more Korean companies at random, 75 from each quarter of the rest by market cap; US-A to US-D = 100 US companies at random, 25 from each quarter. ETFs, funds and preferred shares are left out (a preferred share uses its common share's logo). "All (est.)" rows weight each group by its share of all companies; ± is a 95% range.
  • Reading the table: the big number is the share of the group's companies with a logo by that route (a company without that identifier counts as no logo); the small one is logos / loads tested so far ("…" while loading). Best mix = any route found a logo.
  • Thumbnails: look for wrong or generic logos (a group logo, a placeholder). The bordered one is the best mix's pick.
