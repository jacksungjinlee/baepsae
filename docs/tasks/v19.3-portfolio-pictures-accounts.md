## Part F — 가진 종목 넣기 (board `Import2`)

- Stepper: 종목 넣기 → 기준 → 점검. The 기준 step is the Part D card screen; 빼기 and 조건 are skipped in this flow.
- Search by Korean name, English name, 6-digit code or US ticker, Korea and US. Each added row: logo, name, market
  (the exchange name where the data has one, otherwise 한국 / 미국), quantity, value in 원 and weight, and either its
  평균 단가 or "+ 평균 단가 넣기 (선택)".
- **붙여 넣기 / 여러 종목 한 번에:** one stock per line, name or code then quantity. Brokerage copies often carry several
  numbers per line and "1,234" clashes with comma separators, so: show a **preview** of what was read (stock, quantity)
  and add nothing until the user confirms; a line with no clear single quantity goes to the unreadable list ("{n}번째
  줄은 수량을 읽지 못해 넣지 않았어요."); a line with no matching stock likewise. Never guess a stock from a partial name
  when two or more match; ask the user to pick.
- **금액으로 넣기 / 수량 대신 금액:** the user types an amount instead of a quantity. This is also how someone builds a
  portfolio fully by hand (Jack, 2026-11-05). Store the amount **with that day's close and date**, and derive a decimal
  quantity from them, so the value moves with the price afterwards and never shows a frozen amount as if it were live.
  No purchase price is invented.
- Quantities: whole shares for Korea; US may have decimals (some Korean brokers sell fractional US shares).
- **평균 단가 stays optional.** Never fill it with today's price or any other guess (it would show a fake 0% return).
  Store it as typed, in the stock's currency.
- **Stocks the 기준 can't score** (preferred shares, ETFs, US listings outside the universe, anything without data) can
  still be added: they count in value, weights and the concentration rules, are left out of 기준 averages, and are named
  under the report headline ("기준 점수를 매길 수 없는 종목 {n}개({names})는 비중에만 넣었어요."). A stock not in the
  data at all can be added by name with an amount only, marked the same way.
- Donut of value by holding in the Part C shades, with the total in the middle ("{n}종목", amount in 만원 style), and
  the date line "{date} 종가 · 미국 종목은 1달러 {fx}원으로 계산".

## Part G — 내 포트폴리오 점검 (board `Report2`)

out of averages as the engine does. If the data is stale, the report opens with "데이터가 {n}일째 갱신되지 않았어요.
{date} 종가 기준 결과예요."

3. **종목별로 보면:** rows = holdings (logo, name, weight bar), columns = chosen 기준 (picture + name header; more than four

4. **어느 업종에 몰려 있나요?:** treemap by industry group (the v17.5.1 groups shown on company pages; US mapped into the

   배당을 1년치로 환산한 값이에요."
7. **비중을 바꿔 보면:** a sandbox. It starts at the user's current weights and changes **only** when the user moves a
