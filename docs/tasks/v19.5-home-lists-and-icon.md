## Part A — 홈 "종목 순위"

Jack wants more than 거래대금 on 홈, and the logos to move like logo.dev's "Transaction Enrichment API" showcase.
That showcase (checked in a browser, 2026-11-06) is a list duplicated twice inside a 192 px window, moved with
`@keyframes { 0% { translateY(0) } 100% { translateY(-50%) } }`, 24 s, linear, infinite, and switched off under
`prefers-reduced-motion`. Ours is the same idea, made calmer and usable:

- **Where:** 홈, below the 시장 tiles, in the v19.3e 홈 layout. Visitors without a portfolio get it too: from v19.3c the
  first visit opens the welcome screen (`Guide2a`, which carries 뱁이 and no numbers), so the no-portfolio 홈 has **no
  뱁이** and shows the full card with numbers (CLAUDE.md: 뱁이 never on a screen with numbers). Until v19.3c ships,
  today's names-only first-visit list stays as it is. "전체 보기" opens the 종목 tab with the same chip selected, as a
  still (non-moving) list of 30.
- **Header:** "종목 순위" (not "오늘의 종목", which Korean media use for stock-pick columns), the content date, a 한국 /
  미국 switch. Date line per list: price-based lists "{date} 종가"; lists built on report numbers (기준별, 내 기준,
  Korean 배당수익률) "{date} 종가 · {year}년 사업보고서 기준" (both approved); US lists "{date} 종가 (미국 시간)".
- **Seven chips, in Jack's order:** 시가총액 · 거래대금 · 거래 급증 · 기준별 · 내 기준 · 배당수익률 · 업종별 등락.
  The first chip is selected on first open; afterwards the last chip used is remembered on the device (try/catch).
  The selected chip scrolls into view. 내 기준 shows only when the user has a portfolio with 기준 points. Under 미국,
  거래 급증 is hidden (US data has no daily trading value or 20-day average).
- **One line under the chips** says what the order means (Appendix 1).
- **The rolling list:** top 10, rows 58 px (rank, logo, name, industry, the list's number, today's change in small
  red/blue). Window shows 5 rows with soft fade at top and bottom.
  - Motion: 32 s per loop, linear, holding about 2 s with #1 at the top (`0%, 7% { translateY(0) }`), two copies of
    the list (the second `aria-hidden`).
  - Stops while touched, hovered or focused; stops when off-screen (IntersectionObserver) or when the tab is hidden;
    no motion at all under `prefers-reduced-motion` (a still list of 5 with "전체 보기").
  - The rows stay real links to each company page; screen readers get the list once, not the moving copy.
- **Lists** (each ranked **within its market**; ETFs, preferred shares and stocks under the 5억 trading-value floor left
  out; a stock missing the list's number is left out, never ranked as 0; CLAUDE.md "never rank by a blank field"):
  1. **시가총액:** `cap`, largest first. Number: market cap in the existing format.
  2. **거래대금:** `val`, largest first (today's existing list). US `val` is Yahoo's 3-month daily average, so under 미국
     the line reads "최근 3개월 하루 평균 거래대금이 큰 순서예요."
  3. **거래 급증 (Korea only):** `val ÷ val20`, only when at least 2× and only companies with 시가총액 3,000억원 이상
     (without the floor, the list is mostly small caps up 10–30% and reads like a "hot stocks" screen). Largest first.
     Number "평소의 {x}배". None → the empty line in Appendix 1.
  4. **기준별:** a second chip row with the 기준 pictures: 가치 · 수익성 · 재무 안정 · 낮은 변동성 · 추세 · 성장. Card
     scores tie often (11 Korean stocks tie at the top of 추세 today), so rank by **the real value the card's basis line
     names** (가치 → PBR, 수익성 → ROE, 재무 안정 → 부채 부담, 낮은 변동성 → 주가 변동, 추세 → 최근 1년 수익률, 성장 → 매출
     증가율), with the card's direction and 해당 없음 rules, ties broken by market cap, and show that value on each row.
     If a card's score blends more than the named field, say so in the PR. 배당 and 대형주 are left out of this row
     because 배당수익률 and 시가총액 are their own chips. Pending cards don't appear.
  5. **내 기준:** the latest portfolio **that has 기준 points**: its points and its 빼기 settings apply; its strictness and
     its 한국/미국 mix don't (the switch picks the market, and the list is a top 10 anyway). Ranked with the build step's
     engine within the selected market. Row number: "내 기준 상위 {x}%" (the same percentile the company page uses).
     A Korea-only portfolio still gets a US list under 미국 (the same points applied to US stocks).
  6. **배당수익률:** Korea uses only verified today's-price yields (`dyt`, PR #50; unverified companies left out), US uses
     Yahoo's current dividend rate with the line "미국 종목은 최근 배당을 1년치로 환산한 값이에요." (not a forecast). A company with a 재무 경고 signal keeps its
     place but shows a small "재무 경고" chip on its row (high yields often follow a price crash).
  7. **업종별 등락:** rows are industry groups (v17.5.1 groups), each with the logos of its three largest companies
     overlapped, "{n}개 중 {k}개 오름/내림", and the group's average change today computed **exactly like 왜 움직였나's
     업종 평균** (so the two screens never disagree); highest first. Groups with fewer than 5 priced companies are left
     out. Tapping a row shows that group's companies by market cap in the same window; no separate industry screen yet
     (it comes with 조건으로 찾기, v19.1c), so no "업종 안 종목 보기" link for now.
- **Stale or failed data:** the date line always shows the real content date; when the app's existing stale-data rule
  (the one the date line uses) says the data is old, the list doesn't move. Load failure → the existing load-failure
  pattern, never an empty box.
- **Proposed in the build PR for Jack's yes:** number formats (e.g. "52.1조"), empty-state lines, and the test changes.
- PC (≥1024 px): the same card in 홈's right column, 7 rows visible.
