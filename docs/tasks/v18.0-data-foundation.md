## Part A — Fix three live data bugs (first PR, small)

Found in the data published on 2026-10-31. Check first whether v17.5 already fixed any; if so, prove it with a test.

1. **"nan ·" in company descriptions.** 867 Korean rows have `dk` starting with the literal text "nan ·" (a missing
   KSIC text turned into a string). Fix the cause, and add a publication-gate rule: no user-facing string field may
   contain the words `nan`, `NaN`, `None`, `null` or `undefined`.
2. **Industry groups are too rough to compare peers.** The keyword map yields 31 labels, 353 Korean stocks in `etc`,
   and 삼성전자 as `machine`. 왜 움직였나 will say "업종 12개 중 11개가 빠졌어요", so a wrong group is a wrong fact.
   - Replace keyword matching with the KSIC code from DART's company API (`induty_code`) mapped to about 50–70
     investor-facing industry groups with Korean names. Commit the mapping as a reviewed table
     (`data/industry_map.csv`: KSIC prefix → group id → Korean name).
   - US: SEC SIC codes (EDGAR submissions) mapped into the same groups where they match.
   - Must-have assertions (gate): 삼성전자, SK하이닉스 → 반도체; 현대차, 기아 → 자동차; KB금융, 하나금융지주 → 은행;
     ungrouped ≤ 2% of the liquid Korean universe; every group used for peer comparison has ≥ 5 members (else merge).
3. **52-week high/low sanity.** 현대차 shows `hi` 750,000 against a price of 278,500 and `r1` −20.5%. Find the cause
   (mixed adjusted/unadjusted series, a bad bar, a share class mix-up). Gate rule: `lo ≤ price ≤ hi`, and `hi`/`lo`
   come from the same official-close series as `price`. Log any stock with `hi ÷ price > 3` for review.

## Part B — Price and risk history

- 10 years of daily **official closes** for the Korean universe (built from final listings, Marcap ÷ Stocks, as v17.5
  established) and US stocks (split-adjusted for returns, unadjusted kept for display). One `pxDate` per market.
- Monthly series from the daily ones. From them: 3-year monthly volatility, beta and correlation to the stock's own
  market index (KOSPI/KOSDAQ or S&P 500). Store the window with every number.
- 20-day average daily trading value (for the liquidity floor and position caps).
- Storage: history is large. Keep it out of the files the app loads on start. Propose the layout in Plan mode (for
  example per-stock or sharded files loaded on demand, cached by the workflow), with a size budget for what the app
  downloads on first open.

## Part C — Fundamentals history

- Korea (OpenDART, from 2015): annual and quarterly revenue, operating profit, net income, equity, total liabilities,
  operating / investing / financing cash flow, DPS by period, treasury shares acquired and cancelled.
- US (SEC company facts, XBRL): the same items where reported.
- Derived per stock: years of consecutive operating-profit growth, **years of consecutive DPS increase**, payout ratio,
  net debt, ROE, shareholder-return ratio ((dividends + buybacks) ÷ net income), and **own-history position**:
  where today's P/E, PBR and dividend yield sit within the stock's own 5- and 10-year range (percentile).
- Each derived value stores the periods it used and the filing date.

## Part D — Factor table (feeds the 10 criteria cards)

Ten factors, each a percentile **within its own market** (KR and US ranked separately):
가치 (PER·PBR), 배당 (dividend yield), 배당 성장 (years of DPS increase), 성장 (revenue + operating-profit growth),
수익성 (ROE), 재무 안정 (debt ratio, inverted), 낮은 변동성 (3-year monthly volatility, inverted), 추세 (12-month return),
주주환원 (shareholder-return ratio), 대형주 (market cap).
- A blank input means the stock has no score for that factor. Never rank a blank as zero or as the median. Log coverage
  per factor ("· 배당 성장 계산: 1,212 / 1,362").
- Gate: coverage ≥ the threshold Jack approves after the first real run (propose numbers with evidence).

## Part F — Dividend and earnings calendar

- Korea: dividend record date, pay date, DPS and frequency from DART 현금·현물배당 결정 filings. Detect frequency changes
  (annual → quarterly) instead of assuming last year's pattern.
- Korea earnings dates: from 영업(잠정)실적 공정공시 and IR 개최 filings when announced. Otherwise an estimate from last
  year's same-quarter date, stored with `estimated: true` and its basis. The app must label it 예상.
- US: dividends from company facts/declarations; earnings dates from a named source, stored with that source.

## Part G — Daily event feed (no AI)

From filing **types** alone (no text reading):
- Korea (DART): 공개매수신고서, 합병·분할 결정, 자기주식 취득·처분·소각 결정, 기업가치 제고 계획, 감사보고서 (opinion
  other than 적정), 횡령·배임 혐의 발생, 현금·현물배당 결정 (with **cut detection** against the prior comparable period,
  ignoring special dividends), 최대주주 변경, 관리종목 지정, 투자주의·경고·위험 지정.
- US (EDGAR): 8-K item codes for material agreements, completed acquisitions, executive changes, auditor changes, plus
  SC TO-T (tender offers) and SC 13D (activists).
- Output: event code, stock, date, filing link. These feed the common red flags and the "half size for pending events"
  rule.
- Same-industry move stats for 왜 움직였나: per group per day, how many fell, the group average, each stock's position,
  and for each stock the dates in the last 3 years with a fall of 6% or more and whether its group also fell that day.
