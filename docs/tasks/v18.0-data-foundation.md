## Part A — Fix live data bugs (first PR)

Found in the data published on 2026-10-31. Check first whether v17.5 already fixed any; prove each fix with a test.

1. **Company descriptions are built from shifted columns.**
   - 867 Korean rows start with the literal text "nan ·";
   - 1,609 start with "우량기업부 ·" (the market section, not a business);
   - all 2,476 show "주요제품: <industry name>" (the industry, not the products).

   Remap the source columns. Gate rule: no user-facing string may contain the **whole words** `nan`, `NaN`, `None`,
   `null` or `undefined` (whole-word match, so "Financial" passes).
2. **Industry groups are too rough to compare peers.** The keyword map yields 31 labels, 353 Korean stocks in `etc`, and
   삼성전자 as `machine`. 왜 움직였나 will say "업종 12개 중 11개가 빠졌어요", so a wrong group is a wrong fact.
   - Map the KSIC code from DART's company API (`induty_code`) to about 50–70 investor-facing groups with Korean names:
     `data/industry_map.csv` (KSIC prefix → group id → Korean name).
   - KSIC alone is not enough: 삼성전자 is "통신 및 방송 장비 제조업", and bank holding companies share the "기타 금융업"
     code with SK and LG. Add `data/industry_overrides.csv` (stock code → group id → one-line reason), reviewed in the PR.
   - US: SEC SIC codes (EDGAR submissions) mapped into the same groups where they match.
   - Must-have assertions (gate): 삼성전자, SK하이닉스 → 반도체; 현대차, 기아 → 자동차; KB금융, 하나금융지주 → 은행;
     SK, LG → 지주회사; ungrouped ≤ 2% of the liquid Korean universe; every group used for peer comparison has ≥ 5
     members (else merge it into its parent).
3. **52-week high/low: check, don't assume a bug.** This item first claimed 현대차's `hi` of 750,000 (price 278,500) was
   wrong. v17.5 found it is a real KRX close (2026-06-01, no split), and the 10/31 data shows a market-wide fall
   (median `hi ÷ price` 1.89; 353 Korean stocks above 3). So a high ratio is not evidence of a bug, and the earlier
   "`hi ÷ price > 3` needs a logged reason" rule is dropped.
   - Recompute `hi`/`lo` from the split-adjusted official-close series cut at `pxDate` (Part B), and log every stock
     whose value changes, with the reason (split, share class, bad bar).
   - Gate: must-have stocks' `hi`/`lo` within 1% of an independent source.

## Part B — Price and risk history

- **Source:** the listing cache only goes back to 2026-03-09, so history cannot be built from it. Use a split-adjusted
  daily series per stock (name the source, with a fallback, and log which was used), **cut at `pxDate`**. The latest
  day must equal the official close as merged v17.5 defines it; assert that for the must-have stocks.
- US: split-adjusted for returns; unadjusted kept for display.
- Monthly series from the daily ones. From them: 3-year monthly volatility, beta to the stock's own market index
  (KOSPI/KOSDAQ or S&P 500) **with its estimation-error band** (CLAUDE.md), and correlation. Store the window with every
  number.
- 20-day average daily trading value (for the liquidity floor and position caps).
- Storage: keep history out of the files the app loads on start. Propose the layout in Plan mode (for example sharded
  files loaded on demand), with a size budget for the app's first-open download.

## Part C — Fundamentals history

- Korea (OpenDART, from 2015): annual and quarterly revenue, operating profit, net income, equity, total liabilities,
  operating / investing / financing cash flow, DPS by period (DART's structured dividend API), treasury shares acquired
  and cancelled.
- **Call budget:** one daily run is already close to OpenDART's limit (about 20,000 calls a day). Backfill over several
  days with a fixed per-run budget, cache results, then fetch only new filings. Log calls used per run. Never let the
  backfill starve the daily run.
- US (SEC company facts, XBRL): the same items where reported. SEC requires a contact in the User-Agent: read it from an
  Actions secret (`SEC_USER_AGENT`). Never write an email address into the repo.
- Derived per stock: years of consecutive operating-profit growth, **years of consecutive DPS increase**, payout ratio,
  net debt, ROE, shareholder-return ratio ((dividends + buybacks) ÷ net income), and **own-history position** (where
  today's P/E, PBR and dividend yield sit in the stock's own 5- and 10-year range). Store the periods and filing date used.

## Part D — Factor table (feeds the 10 criteria cards)

Ten factors, each a percentile **within its own market** (KR and US ranked separately):
가치 (PER·PBR), 배당 (dividend yield), 배당 성장 (years of DPS increase), 성장 (revenue + operating-profit growth),
수익성 (ROE), 재무 안정 (debt ratio, inverted), 낮은 변동성 (3-year monthly volatility, inverted), 추세 (12-month return),
주주환원 (shareholder-return ratio), 대형주 (market cap).
- A blank input means no score for that factor. Never rank a blank as zero or the median. Log coverage per factor
  ("· 배당 성장 계산: 1,212 / 1,362").
- Financial companies: say which factors don't apply (e.g. debt ratio for banks) instead of scoring them.

## Part E — Life stage

Per `REDESIGN.md` §4.4, from the latest annual cash-flow signs. Banks, insurers and brokers get `none`. Store the three
signs for the receipt. Log coverage.

## Part F — Dividend and earnings calendar

For every item, name a source reachable from GitHub's runners (KRX sites block them), with a fallback. If an item has no
reachable source, mark it "not available" in the data; never fill it silently.
- Korea dividends: record date, pay date, DPS and frequency from DART dividend-decision filings (parse the filing
  document's table; no AI). Detect frequency changes (annual → quarterly) instead of assuming last year's pattern.
- Korea earnings dates: from 영업(잠정)실적 and IR 개최 filings when announced. Otherwise an estimate from last year's
  same-quarter date, stored with `estimated: true` and its basis; the app labels it 예상.
- US: dividends from company facts/declarations; earnings dates from a named source.

## Part G — Daily event feed (no AI; structured APIs or table parsing only)

- Korea (DART): 공개매수, 합병·분할, 자기주식 취득·처분·소각, 기업가치 제고 계획, audit opinion other than 적정 (DART's
  audit-opinion API), 횡령·배임 혐의 발생, 최대주주 변경, dividend decisions with **cut detection** against the prior
  comparable period (ignore special dividends; handle frequency changes), 관리종목 and 투자주의·경고·위험 designations
  (find a reachable source or mark the 빼기 switch "data not available").
- US (EDGAR): 8-K item codes for material agreements, completed acquisitions, executive and auditor changes;
  tender offers (SC TO-T) and activist stakes (form type `SCHEDULE 13D`; check current EDGAR form names).
- Output per event: code, stock, filing date, link.
- Same-industry move stats for 왜 움직였나: per group per day, how many fell, the group average, each stock's position, and
  for each stock the dates in the last 3 years when it moved by the big-move threshold or more (±5%, `REDESIGN.md` §2)
  and whether its group also moved that day.
