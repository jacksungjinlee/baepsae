## Why

Today a portfolio can't change after it's made: there's no way to add a stock, take one out, or replace one. Jack
(2026-11-07): users add stocks to spread risk or reach another sector, and remove them when the reason they held them
is gone, a rule they set broke, or they found a company that fits better. They should tell 뱁새 what they sold, when
and how much (v19.6 Part A), and use 뱁새 to find candidates that fit the portfolio's own 기준.

**Product rule (CLAUDE.md, locked):** 뱁새 never promises returns or recommends a stock. Jack asked for candidates that
"offer better expected returns". This design keeps his intent inside the rule: candidates are ranked by **the
portfolio's own 기준 points**, and comparisons show **facts** (내 기준 rank, past price swings, β band, valuation, dividend,
what today's price assumes, 재무 경고). No line says a stock will earn more.

## Part A — 종목 바꾸기 (board `PfEdit3`)

- Entry: on the 포트폴리오 screen, a "종목 바꾸기" button next to the holdings list title, for every portfolio.
- [B2] **Built portfolios** (they have targets and per-stock rules) get this screen. **Imported portfolios** (v19.3d: no
  targets, no per-stock rules) get no target steppers: 종목 바꾸기 opens v19.3d's 가진 종목 넣기 screen with the current
  holdings, where the user adds stocks, changes quantity or amount, and removes rows, then runs 점검 again. [C1] This is an
  **edit in place**, not a new import: rows the user doesn't touch keep every stored field (quantity or amount with its
  saved close and date, 평균 단가) and all their records; only a changed row takes today's close and date; a new row
  takes today's close and date. Nothing is re-priced and no record is lost (test: open, change nothing, save → the
  store is byte-for-byte the same apart from edit times). Removing a row
  that has shares uses Part B's sheet (reasons; 팔고 빼기 records a sale). 내 기준으로 찾기 (Part D) works for both; for an
  imported portfolio, + 담기 adds a row to 가진 종목 넣기 for the user to fill in.
- **업종 비중:** a stacked bar of target weights by industry group (the v17.5.1 groups 업종별 등락 uses), with a legend.
  It updates live as the user edits.
- **목표 비중 list:** each holding with its logo, name, "지금 {qty}주 · {industry}", a − / + stepper for its target
  (1%-point steps), and 빼기. A holding whose rule is broken today shows the existing 규칙 깨짐 line for that rule (the
  same words the 포트폴리오 screen uses) under its row, so the user sees why it might go.
- **합계:** the sum of targets. When it isn't 100%, it shows in the watch colour with the button "합계를 100%로
  맞추기", which scales the other holdings proportionally (the one just changed keeps its value). Save is enabled only
  at 100%.
- **+ 종목 더하기:** two ways: 이름으로 찾기 (Part C) and 내 기준으로 찾기 (Part D).
- **바꾼 내용 저장:** writes the new targets. For every holding whose target changed, regenerate its band rule from the
  new target (the engine's band rule with `BANDS`); every other rule of every holding stays as it is. [B1, corrected by
  C3] Today the band is measured from the planned share count, so a changed target would silently stop being checked
  (cut A from 25% to 10% while A sits at 25%, and nothing is flagged). **Keep every `planPx`** (dropping one would shift
  every other holding's band). For a holding whose target changes, recompute its `plannedQty` from the new target at its
  own `planPx`, so its band follows the new target and nobody else's plan values move. A stock added with 담기 gets
  `planPx` = today's official close and a `plannedQty` from its target. Say in the PR if the plan total needs care.
  Test the 25% → 10% case: it must be flagged the same day, and the other holdings' bands must not change. The approved line
  under the button says the band rules follow the new targets. Nothing is saved until the user taps it; leaving asks
  "바꾼 내용을 저장하지 않고 나갈까요?".
- [C2] Built portfolios have no daily 한 종목 최대 % check, so the editor enforces it: the + stepper stops at
  `settings.maxPct`, and the row says why ("이 포트폴리오는 한 종목 최대 비중이 {x}%라서 더 올릴 수 없어요."). 담기 and 합계를
  100%로 맞추기 never push a holding above it either; if they would, the share-out caps it and spreads the rest.
- [D2] **When the cap can't reach 100%** (Jack, 2026-11-08). With n holdings and a cap of x% (`settings.maxPct`), the
  targets can reach 100% only if n × x ≥ 100. When **n × x < 100** (e.g. a 5-stock portfolio at a 20% cap after one
  removal: 4 × 20 = 80):
  - 바꾼 내용 저장 is off. In place of 합계를 100%로 맞추기 (which can't reach 100% here) the screen shows "종목이
    {n}개뿐이라, 한 종목을 최대 {x}%까지만 담으면 합계가 100%가 되지 않아요." and the button "최대 비중을 {y}%로 올리기".
  - n = the holdings in the edited list (after pending removals and 담기); x = this portfolio's 최대 비중, a whole
    number (the build offers 10/15/20/25). y = 100 ÷ n **rounded up** to a whole number: 3 → 34, 4 → 25, 6 → 17,
    7 → 15, 9 → 12. Compare and compute in whole numbers. The check runs only for built portfolios that have a
    `settings.maxPct` (imported portfolios have no targets).
  - The button sets this portfolio's `settings.maxPct` to y (from then on the + stepper stops at y), then shares out
    what's missing to 100% in proportion under the new cap, as 합계를 100%로 맞추기 does. It is a pending edit like the
    steppers: 바꾼 내용 저장 saves it; leaving without saving drops it (and asks the usual question). On save, as for
    any target change (C3), every `planPx` stays; each holding whose target changed gets its `plannedQty` recomputed
    from the new target at its own `planPx` and its band rule regenerated; the others keep everything.
  - Adding a stock (담기) also clears it: once n × x ≥ 100, the line and button go and 합계를 100%로 맞추기 is back.
  - 팔고 빼기 saves at once (B4), so its sheet checks first with n = the holdings left after the removal. If n × x <
    100, the 빼기 sheet shows the same line and button, and 팔고 빼기 stays off until the user taps the button. The raised
    cap then saves together with the sale, the removal and the share-out, as one action; cancelling the sheet drops it.
    기록 없이 빼기 and 빼기 (no shares) wait for 바꾼 내용 저장, so after them the editor shows the line. A swap (Part
    E) keeps n, so it never triggers this.
  - Edge cases. n × x = 100 exactly (5 at 20%, 4 at 25%): no line; every holding can sit at the cap and 합계를 100%로
    맞추기 reaches 100%. n = 0 can't happen (Part B: the last holding can't be removed); the code still never divides by
    0: with no holdings it shows no line and no button, and save stays off. y can't be above 100: n ≥ 1, so y ≤ 100
    (n = 1 gives 100); the code still caps y at 100. y is always above x, because x < 100 ÷ n ≤ y.
  - Test: 4 holdings at a 20% cap shows the line with y = 25 and save off; the button gives 25% each and save on, and
    the saved holdings keep their `planPx`; 5 at 20% shows no line; 팔고 빼기 from 5 holdings at 20% is off until the
    button is tapped, then saves the sale, the removal, maxPct 25 and targets summing to 100% in one write.
- [B4] **What saves when:** anything that records money saves at once, together with the change it belongs to. 팔고
  빼기 saves the sale, the removal and the proportional share-out of its target the moment the sale is saved; a swap
  (Part E) saves the sale, the removal and the candidate's add with the old target as one action. Everything else (target
  steppers, 담기, 기록 없이 빼기) waits for 바꾼 내용 저장, and leaving asks "바꾼 내용을 저장하지 않고 나갈까요?" only when
  such edits are pending.
- A change log is kept per portfolio, `changes: [{ at, kind: "add"|"remove"|"targets", t?, reason? }]` (at most 200,
  oldest dropped), shown in the portfolio's 기록 list ("{date} · {name} 뺌 · {reason}").

## Part B — 빼기 (board `PfRemove3`)

- Tapping 빼기 opens a sheet: "{name}을/를 뺄까요?", the held quantity and 평균 단가, and the 규칙 깨짐 line if one fires.
- **왜 빼나요? (선택):** four chips, one choice, optional: 산 이유가 사라졌어요 · 규칙이 깨졌어요 · 더 잘 맞는 종목을
  찾았어요 · 기타. Stored as `reason` (`thesis`, `rule`, `better`, `other`) on the change and on the stock's `history`
  entry (v19.6 A.3). Preselect 규칙이 깨졌어요 when a rule fires, and 더 잘 맞는 종목을 찾았어요 when the user came from a swap
  (Part E).
- **With shares held:**
  - 팔고 빼기 · 판 내용 기록 → v19.6's 팔았어요 sheet, prefilled with all held shares. After the sale is saved, the stock
    leaves the portfolio and its records move to `history` (v19.6 A.3). Cancelling the sale cancels the removal.
  - 기록 없이 빼기 → the stock leaves; sale and dividend records move to `history`; unsold shares leave 내 수익 with their
    cost (v19.6 A.3), as the approved note says.
- **With no shares** (planned, never bought): one button, 빼기.
- Its target is shared out to the remaining holdings in proportion (the 합계 stays 100%; when the cap makes that
  impossible, Part A [D2] applies); the user can adjust before saving.
- The last holding can't be removed (a portfolio needs at least one stock): its 빼기 is disabled with the line "종목이
  하나뿐이라 뺄 수 없어요."

## Part C — 이름으로 찾기 (add one stock)

- The existing stock search (the same one the 종목 tab uses), filtered to stocks not already in this portfolio.
  Stocks the engine can't score (ETFs, preferred shares, unknown) can be added as "직접 추가" (the existing build-step
  label and note), as in v19.3.
- Picking a stock opens 담기: its logo, name, industry, its 내 기준 rank for this portfolio (when the portfolio has 기준
  points), 재무 경고 count, and a 목표 비중 stepper defaulting to an equal share (100 ÷ (holdings + 1), rounded), with
  the others scaled proportionally and the 업종 비중 bar after the change.
- 담기 adds it as a planned holding (no purchase), with the rules the build step would give it (`rulesFor` / `ruleBook`
  with this portfolio's `settings.points` and the new target), `kind` as the build step sets it, `setAt` today. The user
  records a purchase later with v19.6's 기록 → 샀어요.
- Mixed-market rules: a US stock in a Korea-only portfolio is allowed (the user's choice); the 한국/미국 mix shown on the
  portfolio updates.

## Part D — 내 기준으로 찾기 (board `PfFind3`, v19.7b)

- Uses this portfolio's saved `settings` (points, 빼기 settings `exclude`, strictness `strict`, market `mix`), ranked by
  the build engine (`screen()`), excluding stocks already in the portfolio.
- [B3, text fixed by D1] **One 내 기준 number per stock, "상위 {x}%":** the stock's weighted 기준 score (this portfolio's
  points) as a percentile among all scorable stocks of its market, computed **before** 빼기 and the finder's filters, so
  a stock a filter drops still has a number elsewhere. A stock that can't be scored shows "—", never 0. One function
  computes it, and it is used **only** where one number per stock is shown: this finder, the compare screen (Part E:
  its 내 기준 row, its verdict and its default holding), 종목 바꾸기's 담기 sheet (Part C) and v19.5b's 내 기준 list on
  홈 (this replaces v19.5's "the same percentile the company page uses", which doesn't exist: the company page ranks
  per 기준). Part A's 목표 비중 rows don't show it (the board and Appendix 1 have no place for it). **점검 stays as it
  is** (Jack, 2026-11-08): it keeps ranking each 기준 separately and doesn't use this number.
- **A portfolio without 기준 points** (imported without 기준, or older): show "이 포트폴리오에는 기준 점수가 없어요. 찾기
  전에 기준을 골라 주세요." and the 기준 cards (`BuildCards2`); the chosen points are saved to that portfolio's
  `settings.points`.
- Header card: the portfolio's 기준 with their points (picture chip, name, dots).
- Filter chips, one at a time:
  - 전체: the top 20 by rank.
  - 없는 업종: only industry groups with 0% target in this portfolio, top 20.
  - 주가 변동 작은 순: the top 20% by rank, sorted by `vol` (lowest first), top 20. Blank `vol` is left out.
  - 배당 주는 종목: verified dividend payers (Korea `dyt`, US as v19.5's list), top 20 by rank.
- Each row: rank, logo, name, "{industry} · 연 변동성 {vol}%", "상위 {x}%", a 새 업종 tag when its group is at 0% in the
  portfolio, and two actions: 비교 (Part E) and + 담기 (Part C's 담기 sheet). Tapping the name opens the company page.
- Date line: "{date} 종가 · {year}년 사업보고서 기준" (approved in v19.5). Under 없는 업종, the approved line saying
  only sectors not in the portfolio are shown.
- No line anywhere says a candidate will earn more.
- [B5] **Empty and stale:** 0 results → "이 조건에 맞는 종목이 없어요."; 없는 업종 with nothing left → "지금 포트폴리오에
  없는 업종에서는 고를 종목이 없어요."; no 뱁이 (the screen shows scores). Stale data: the finder, 담기 and 비교 still work,
  with the real date line and the existing stale warning, as every other screen. Failed load: the existing load-failure
  message instead of the list.

## Part E — 두 종목 비교 · 바꾸기 (board `PfCompare3`, v19.7b)

- Opened from 비교. It compares the candidate with one holding: by default the holding with a broken rule, else the one
  with the lowest 내 기준 rank. "어떤 종목과 비교할까요?" lets the user pick another holding; with one holding, the
  picker is hidden [B5].
- **Verdict line** (cards lead with the conclusion): built only from two facts, 내 기준 rank and past price swings
  (`vol`), using the four approved variants in Appendix 1. When either fact is blank, use the single-fact variant. It
  never mentions returns.
- **Rows** (holding | candidate), plain values, no colour judging: 내 기준 (상위 x%) · 업종 (+ 새 업종) · 주가 변동 (지난 1년,
  `vol`) · 코스피 1% → 평균 {β band} (v19.3 Part O rules; US with S&P 500; "자료가 없어요" when unreliable) · PBR · 배당수익률
  (verified only, else "—") · 오늘 주가가 가정하는 성장 (`impliedGrowth`, the company page's own number and edge
  messages) · 재무 경고 신호 (count; "확인한" wording rules from v19.1b). Under the table, the approved β note.
- **바꾸면 업종 비중:** the bar after the swap and "{group} {a}% → {b}% · 업종 {n}개 → {m}개" for the largest change.
- **Buttons:** "{candidate}만 담기" → Part C's 담기 sheet. "{holding}과/와 바꾸기" → Part B's sheet for the holding with
  더 잘 맞는 종목을 찾았어요 preselected; after it completes, the candidate is added with the holding's target. The approved
  line under the buttons explains both.

## Store and rules

- New per-portfolio fields: `changes` (Part A). `history` entries gain `removedAt` and optional `reason` (Part B).
  Validation and round-trips as v19.6 A.1 (store v2; no version bump of its own if it ships after v19.6a, otherwise
  bump and test like v19.6).
- Every place that reads holdings (rules, money, KOSPI comparison, 점검, 홈) must handle a holding added after the
  portfolio was made (its `setAt` is later than `createdAt`) and a removed one (gone from `holdings`, records in
  `history`). In particular, the KOSPI comparison and 내 수익 follow the records (v19.6), not the target list.
- Rule checks for a newly added stock start from the day it was added (`setAt`).
