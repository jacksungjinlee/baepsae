# Task v19.7 — 종목 바꾸기: add, remove and swap stocks in a portfolio you already have

Status: TODO

Designed with Jack in claude.ai chat, 2026-11-07 (design board version 56). Boards: `docs/design/v19.7/reference/`
(`PfEdit3` 종목 바꾸기, `PfRemove3` 빼기, `PfFind3` 내 기준으로 찾기, `PfCompare3` 두 종목 비교). Companies and numbers on
the boards are made up. Korean text is final where Appendix 1 lists it (voice guide, DeepL grammar check and
back-translation).

## Why

Today a portfolio can't change after it's made: there's no way to add a stock, take one out, or replace one. Jack
(2026-11-07): users add stocks to spread risk or reach another sector, and remove them when the reason they held them
is gone, a rule they set broke, or they found a company that fits better. They should tell 뱁새 what they sold, when
and how much (v19.6 Part A), and use 뱁새 to find candidates that fit the portfolio's own 기준.

**Product rule (CLAUDE.md, locked):** 뱁새 never promises returns or recommends a stock. Jack asked for candidates that
"offer better expected returns". This design keeps his intent inside the rule: candidates are ranked by **the
portfolio's own 기준 points**, and comparisons show **facts** (내 기준 rank, past price swings, β band, valuation, dividend,
what today's price assumes, 재무 경고). No line says a stock will earn more.

## Part A — 종목 바꾸기 (board `PfEdit3`)

- Entry: on the 포트폴리오 screen, a "종목 바꾸기" button next to the holdings list title, for every portfolio (built or
  imported).
- **업종 비중:** a stacked bar of target weights by industry group (the v17.5.1 groups 업종별 등락 uses), with a legend.
  It updates live as the user edits.
- **목표 비중 list:** each holding with its logo, name, "지금 {qty}주 · {industry}", a − / + stepper for its target
  (1%-point steps), and 빼기. A holding whose rule is broken today shows the existing 규칙 깨짐 line for that rule (the
  same words the 포트폴리오 screen uses) under its row, so the user sees why it might go.
- **합계:** the sum of targets. When it isn't 100%, it shows in the watch colour with the button "합계를 100%로
  맞추기", which scales the other holdings proportionally (the one just changed keeps its value). Save is enabled only
  at 100%.
- **+ 종목 더하기:** two ways: 이름으로 찾기 (Part C) and 내 기준으로 찾기 (Part D).
- **바꾼 내용 저장:** writes the new targets. For every holding whose target changed, regenerate its band rule from the
  new target (the engine's band rule with `BANDS`); every other rule of every holding stays as it is. The approved line
  under the button says the band rules follow the new targets. Nothing is saved until the user taps it; leaving asks
  "바꾼 내용을 저장하지 않고 나갈까요?".
- The portfolio's maximum per stock (`settings.maxPct`) and its own portfolio rules (한 종목 30% 이하, 한 업종 35% 이하 …)
  are not enforced while editing; after saving, the usual daily check reports anything they break. Before saving, a
  target above `maxPct` shows the same 규칙 깨짐 words under its row as a preview.
- A change log is kept per portfolio, `changes: [{ at, kind: "add"|"remove"|"targets", t?, reason? }]` (at most 200,
  oldest dropped), shown in the portfolio's 기록 list ("{date} · {name} 뺌 · {reason}").

## Part B — 빼기 (board `PfRemove3`)

- Tapping 빼기 opens a sheet: "{name}을/를 뺄까요?", the held quantity and 평균 단가, and the 규칙 깨짐 line if one fires.
- **왜 빼나요? (선택):** four chips, one choice, optional: 산 이유가 사라졌어요 · 규칙이 깨졌어요 · 더 잘 맞는 종목을
  찾았어요 · 기타. Stored as `reason` (`thesis`, `rule`, `better`, `other`) on the change and on the stock's `history`
  entry (v19.6 A.3). Preselect 규칙이 깨졌어요 when a rule fires, and 더 잘 맞는 종목을 찾았어요 when the user came from a swap
  (Part E).
- **With shares held:**
  - 팔고 빼기 · 판 내용 기록 → v19.6's 팔았어요 sheet, prefilled with all held shares. After the sale is saved, the stock
    leaves the portfolio and its records move to `history` (v19.6 A.3). Cancelling the sale cancels the removal.
  - 기록 없이 빼기 → the stock leaves; sale and dividend records move to `history`; unsold shares leave 내 수익 with their
    cost (v19.6 A.3), as the approved note says.
- **With no shares** (planned, never bought): one button, 빼기.
- Its target is shared out to the remaining holdings in proportion (the 합계 stays 100%); the user can adjust before
  saving.
- The last holding can't be removed (a portfolio needs at least one stock): its 빼기 is disabled with the line "종목이
  하나뿐이라 뺄 수 없어요."

## Part C — 이름으로 찾기 (add one stock)

- The existing stock search (the same one the 종목 tab uses), filtered to stocks not already in this portfolio.
  Stocks the engine can't score (ETFs, preferred shares, unknown) can be added as "직접 추가" (the existing build-step
  label and note), as in v19.3.
- Picking a stock opens 담기: its logo, name, industry, its 내 기준 rank for this portfolio (when the portfolio has 기준
  points), 재무 경고 count, and a 목표 비중 stepper defaulting to an equal share (100 ÷ (holdings + 1), rounded), with
  the others scaled proportionally and the 업종 비중 bar after the change.
- 담기 adds it as a planned holding (no purchase), with the rules the build step would give it (`rulesFor` / `ruleBook`
  with this portfolio's `settings.points` and the new target), `kind` as the build step sets it, `setAt` today. The user
  records a purchase later with v19.6's 기록 → 샀어요.
- Mixed-market rules: a US stock in a Korea-only portfolio is allowed (the user's choice); the 한국/미국 mix shown on the
  portfolio updates.

## Part D — 내 기준으로 찾기 (board `PfFind3`, v19.7b)

- Uses this portfolio's saved `settings` (points, 빼기 settings `exclude`, strictness `strict`, market `mix`), ranked by
  the build engine (`screen()`), excluding stocks already in the portfolio. The rank shown is the same "상위 {x}%" the
  company page uses.
- **A portfolio without 기준 points** (imported without 기준, or older): show "이 포트폴리오에는 기준 점수가 없어요. 찾기
  전에 기준을 골라 주세요." and the 기준 cards (`BuildCards2`); the chosen points are saved to that portfolio's
  `settings.points`.
- Header card: the portfolio's 기준 with their points (picture chip, name, dots).
- Filter chips, one at a time:
  - 전체: the top 20 by rank.
  - 없는 업종: only industry groups with 0% target in this portfolio, top 20.
  - 주가 변동 작은 순: the top 20% by rank, sorted by `vol` (lowest first), top 20. Blank `vol` is left out.
  - 배당 주는 종목: verified dividend payers (Korea `dyt`, US as v19.5's list), top 20 by rank.
- Each row: rank, logo, name, "{industry} · 연 변동 {vol}%", "상위 {x}%", a 새 업종 tag when its group is at 0% in the
  portfolio, and two actions: 비교 (Part E) and + 담기 (Part C's 담기 sheet). Tapping the name opens the company page.
- Date line: "{date} 종가 · {year}년 사업보고서 기준" (approved in v19.5). Under 없는 업종, the approved line saying
  only sectors not in the portfolio are shown.
- No line anywhere says a candidate will earn more.

## Part E — 두 종목 비교 · 바꾸기 (board `PfCompare3`, v19.7b)

- Opened from 비교. It compares the candidate with one holding: by default the holding with a broken rule, else the one
  with the lowest 내 기준 rank. "어떤 종목과 비교할까요?" lets the user pick another holding.
- **Verdict line** (cards lead with the conclusion): built only from two facts, 내 기준 rank and past price swings
  (`vol`), using the four approved variants in Appendix 1. When either fact is blank, use the single-fact variant. It
  never mentions returns.
- **Rows** (holding | candidate), plain values, no colour judging: 내 기준 (상위 x%) · 업종 (+ 새 업종) · 주가 변동 (지난 1년,
  `vol`) · 코스피 1% → 평균 {β band} (v19.3 Part O rules; US with S&P 500; "자료가 없어요" when unreliable) · PBR · 배당수익률
  (verified only, else "—") · 오늘 주가가 가정하는 성장 (`impliedGrowth`, the company page's own number and edge
  messages) · 재무 경고 신호 (count; "확인한" wording rules from v19.1b). Under the table, the approved β note.
- **바꾸면 업종 비중:** the bar after the swap and "{group} {a}% → {b}% · 업종 {n}개 → {m}개" for the largest change.
- **Buttons:** "{candidate}만 담기" → Part C's 담기 sheet. "{holding}과/와 바꾸기" → Part B's sheet for the holding with
  더 잘 맞는 종목을 찾았어요 preselected; after it completes, the candidate is added with the holding's target. The approved
  line under the buttons explains both.

## Store and rules

- New per-portfolio fields: `changes` (Part A). `history` entries gain `removedAt` and optional `reason` (Part B).
  Validation and round-trips as v19.6 A.1 (store v2; no version bump of its own if it ships after v19.6a, otherwise
  bump and test like v19.6).
- Every place that reads holdings (rules, money, KOSPI comparison, 점검, 홈) must handle a holding added after the
  portfolio was made (its `setAt` is later than `createdAt`) and a removed one (gone from `holdings`, records in
  `history`). In particular, the KOSPI comparison and 내 수익 follow the records (v19.6), not the target list.
- Rule checks for a newly added stock start from the day it was added (`setAt`).
