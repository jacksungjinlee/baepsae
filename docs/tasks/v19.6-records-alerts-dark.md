## Part A — 팔았어요 · 배당 받았어요 (board `Records2`) and 내 수익 (board `Returns2`)

### A.1 Store

Today the store holds only 샀어요 `purchases` (plus v19.3d's imported holdings). Add, per portfolio, optional:

- `sales`: `{ t, qty, price, date, ccy, fx? }`. qty: Korea whole shares; US up to 4 decimals (as v19.3d). price > 0 in the
  stock's own currency. date `YYYY-MM-DD`, not after today (KST), not before the stock's first purchase. `fx` on a USD
  sale: the won-per-dollar rate of the sale date (Part I.1).
- `divs`: `{ t, amount, ccy, date, fx? }`. amount after tax, as the brokerage deposit shows it: Korea in 원, US in
  dollars. `fx` on a USD dividend: the rate of its date.
- `history`: the purchases, sales and dividends of a stock that was taken out of the portfolio (A.3). These records
  need no matching holding.

Validation follows the approach v19.3d settles for imported holdings (one bad record is named and the user's other
portfolios stay readable). Also check, in date order, that no sale takes more shares than were held that day. Old
stores without these parts still read; stores with them round-trip through 기록 불러오기 (JSON) and the v19.4 account
sync. Unit tests for each.

**Held quantity everywhere = bought + imported − sold.** That touches `evaluateDeck`, `deckMoney`, `heldCost`, the
weights and every rule that reads them (줄일 차례, 채울 차례, 추가로 넣을 돈), the 점검 report and the KOSPI comparison.
List every place in the PR and test each with a sale.

**Exchange rate for new purchase records (a fix found while designing this):** 샀어요 saves the data's current rate
(`p.fx`). For a purchase dated in the past, that is the wrong day's rate, so 원금 is off by the rate change. From this
PR, new USD purchase records save the rate of their own date from `fxHist` (Part I.1). Records already saved keep their
rate (no number a user has seen changes without them doing anything). Say in the PR how many code paths saved `fx`.

### A.3 Taking a stock out after selling

`withoutHolding` (v19.0c) deletes the stock's purchases. When the stock has any sale or dividend, move its purchases,
sales and dividends into the portfolio's `history` instead, so 내 수익 keeps them. With no sale or dividend, the old
behaviour stays.

### A.4 Gains

- Cost per share in won: the moving average of purchase cost in won (each purchase at its saved rate), reduced
  proportionally by each sale. This is the 평균 단가 method most Korean brokerage apps show. Brokers differ for tax
  (선입선출 or 이동평균); Part F says so.
- Realized gain of a sale in won = qty × price × sale `fx` − qty × cost per share in won (Korea: `fx` = 1).
- Dividends in won: Korea as typed; US amount × its `fx`.

### A.5 내 수익 (`Returns2`)

- Headline = 가진 종목 (unrealized, as today) + 판 종목 (all realized gains so far) + 받은 배당 (all dividends so far),
  each shown as its own row with the approved second lines ("지금까지 …"). The three rows use the same period (since the
  first record), so they add up.
- Receipt line under the headline (CLAUDE.md: computed ratios show their inputs): 수익률 = the three gains ÷ all money
  put in (including shares later sold). Appendix 1.
- A part that can't be computed (no won cost) is left out and named, as the existing `noFx` line does. Never 0.
- **KOSPI comparison:** stays on price only (the KOSPI series has no dividends), now with sales: each stock's money buys
  KOSPI on its purchase dates (as today), and a sale sells the same fraction of that stock's KOSPI units on the sale
  date. Compare the totals (held + sold). Dividends stay out of both sides. If the existing label could now be read as
  including dividends, propose a one-line note in the PR.
- **미국 종목 수익, 어디서 왔나:** for US stocks still held, split the won gain into 주가 and 환율:
  - per purchase: 주가 = (today's price − purchase price) × qty × purchase rate; 환율 = today's price × qty × (today's
    rate − purchase rate). The two add up exactly to 가진 미국 종목 수익 (unit test).
  - The rate line shows the purchase rate (weighted by dollar cost when there are several purchases: the 평균 line in
    Appendix 1) and today's rate.
  - Holdings without a purchase rate (imported with only 평균 단가) are left out, with the Appendix 1 line.
  - The bar shows only when both parts are 0 or more; otherwise just the two signed numbers.
- **기록 내려받기 (엑셀에서 열려요):** one CSV for all portfolios. UTF-8 with BOM, CRLF line ends, header row in Korean
  (Appendix 1), one row per record (샀어요, 팔았어요, 배당 받았어요, and v19.3d's imported 가진 종목). Dates
  `YYYY-MM-DD`; numbers plain (no thousands separators); text cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or CR get a
  leading `'` (stock and portfolio names are user text). File name `baepsae-records-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`. Export only; it
  is not an import format. Check the download works on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and PC, and under the CSP.

## Part F — 연말에 확인할 숫자 (board `TaxCheck2`)

- Entry: 내 수익 shows "연말에 확인할 숫자 ›" from Nov 1 to Dec 31 (KST) when the user has this year's US sales, US
  holdings or dividend records.

  Appendix 1 line says so; a trade recorded in two portfolios counts twice, which that line covers). The bar runs to
  the 250만원 deduction. Three states (Appendix 1): below, above (with the estimate (gain − 250만원) × 22%, rounded to
  1,000원, with 약), and a net loss. Sales whose gain couldn't be computed are left out and counted in their own line.
- Notes: the approved explanation; the RIA line only in tax year 2026 (the 국내시장 복귀계좌 deduction applies to 2026
  sales); from Dec 15, the settlement-date line.
- **지금 손실 중인 미국 종목:** held US stocks with a negative unrealized won gain, with the approved note. Facts only:
  never suggest selling for tax.
- **올해 받은 배당 (세금 떼기 전):** this year's dividend records turned back into pre-tax amounts (the 2,000만원 test
  uses pre-tax income): Korea ÷ (1 − 0.154), US ÷ (1 − 0.15), shown with 약. The approved note says 예금 이자 isn't
  included.
- Footer: the approved note (rules vary by person and by broker; check with the broker or a 세무사; 2026 tax law).
- Facts behind these lines (checked 2026-11-06, Appendix 2): 22% (20% + local 2%) on yearly overseas gains above
  250만원; gains and losses within the same year net out; brokers use either 선입선출 or 이동평균; a late-December sale
  can fall into the next tax year by its settlement date; the 2,000만원 financial-income threshold counts dividends
