## Part A — 팔았어요 · 배당 받았어요 (board `Records2`) and 내 수익 (board `Returns2`)

### A.1 Store (version 2)

Today the store holds 샀어요 `purchases` (plus v19.3d's imported holdings). Add, per portfolio, optional:

- `sales`: `{ t, qty, price, date, ccy, fx? }`. price > 0 in the stock's own currency. date `YYYY-MM-DD`, not after
  today (KST). `fx` on a USD sale: the won-per-dollar rate of the sale date (Part I.1).
- `divs`: `{ t, amount, ccy, date, fx? }`. amount after tax, as the brokerage deposit shows it: Korea in 원, US in
  dollars. `fx` on a USD dividend: the rate of its date.
- `history`: records of a stock taken out of the portfolio (A.3). These records need no matching holding.

Rules:

- **Quantities** are compared in whole units of 1/10,000 share (Korea is whole shares anyway), never as floating
  sums, so a valid full sale of 0.1 + 0.2 shares is not refused.
- **Held quantity on a date** = imported quantity (v19.3d; an imported holding without a date counts as held before
  every dated record) + purchases dated on or before it − sales dated on or before it. On the same date, purchases
  come before sales. No sale may take the held quantity below 0. Use one shared module for this; Part H's server reuses
  it.
- **Bump `STORE_VERSION` to 2.** A store without the new parts is a valid v2 store (migrate on read). An older client
  that meets a v2 store must not count sold shares as held or overwrite it: check what today's `writeStore` does with
  an unreadable or newer store (it moves it to a backup key and writes a fresh one), and make sure that when an old tab
  and a new tab meet, no record is lost or silently ignored. Test both directions. v19.3 K.3 already stops syncing on a
  newer schema version. [Q5] When the new version finds that an old tab has moved the v2 store aside and written its
  own, it restores the v2 store, merges in what the old tab saved, and shows "다른 창에서 저장한 내용과 기록을 합쳤어요.
  기록을 한 번 확인해 주세요." once.
- [Q6] **기록 불러오기 (file import)** merges as today for portfolios. 관심 종목: the device's list first, then the
  file's in its own order, up to 30; when some don't fit, say how many (Appendix 1). 확인했어요 marks (`seen`): the union,
  keeping the newest time for the same filing.
- Validation follows the approach v19.3d settles for imported holdings (one bad record is named and the user's other
  portfolios stay readable). Validate before every write and after every account merge; a merge that would produce an
  invalid store stops syncing (v19.3 K.3).
- **Deleting a record** that would leave a later sale bigger than the shares held is refused with the Appendix 1 line.
- Old stores still read; v2 stores round-trip through 기록 불러오기 (JSON) and the v19.4 account sync. Unit tests for
  each.

**Held quantity everywhere** = the rule above. That touches `evaluateDeck`, `deckMoney`, `heldCost`, the weights, the
band statuses (trim, high, low), 추가로 넣을 돈, the 점검 report and the KOSPI comparison. List every place in the PR and
test each with a sale.

**A holding sold down to 0 and kept** (남겨 두기) is a "sold out" holding: it counts as held with 0 shares for the band
rules (so it can show as below its band), and it is never treated as "planned, never bought" (today `evaluateDeck`
would put it in `unheld` and show the rebased note).

**Exchange rate for new purchase records (found while designing this):** 샀어요 saves the data's current rate
(`p.fx`). For a purchase dated in the past, that is the wrong day's rate, so 원금 is off by the rate change. From this
PR, a new USD record (purchase, sale, dividend) saves the rate of its own date from `fxHist` (Part I.1); a record dated
today may use data.json `fx` as now. If `fxHist` is missing or doesn't reach that date, a past-dated USD record can't
be saved yet (Appendix 1 line); never save it with another day's rate. Records already saved keep their rate (no
number a user has seen changes without them doing anything). Say in the PR how many code paths saved `fx`.

### A.3 Taking a stock out

`withoutHolding` (v19.0c) deletes the stock's purchases. When the stock has any sale or dividend, move its records
into the portfolio's `history` instead, so 내 수익 keeps its realized gains and dividends. Shares still unsold at that
moment leave the calculations with their cost (the user removed them). With no sale or dividend, the old behaviour
stays.

### A.4 Gains (moving average, the 평균 단가 most Korean brokerage apps show)

- Each purchase is a lot with its won cost (its saved rate, else the portfolio's `planFx`, as `purchaseWon` does).
  A sale reduces **every open lot by the same fraction** (sold ÷ held before the sale). Each lot's remaining quantity
  and remaining won cost carry forward.
- Realized gain of a sale in won = qty × price × sale `fx` − (the won cost the sale removed). Korea: `fx` = 1.
- Imported US holdings with only a dollar 평균 단가 (no rate): v19.3 Part H converts their dollar gain at today's rate
  for the unrealized part, with its approved line. A sale of such a holding: dollar gain × the sale-date rate, with the
  Appendix 1 line saying so. They stay out of the 환율 split (A.5). [Q11] In the 수익률 maths their cost counts at
  today's rate too (dollar 평균 단가 × quantity × today's rate), so their won return equals their dollar return.
- Dividends in won: Korea as typed; US amount × its `fx`.
- `deckMoney`'s cost (원금) becomes the **remaining** cost of open lots, so 포트폴리오 and 내 수익 agree. Say so in the
  PR; the screen label stays.

### A.5 내 수익 (`Returns2`)

- Headline = 가진 종목 (unrealized, as today) + 판 종목 (all realized gains so far) + 받은 배당 (all dividends so far).
  Each row has the approved second line (손익, since they can be negative). The three rows cover the same period (since
  the first record), so they add up. Round each row, then show the headline as the sum of the rounded rows (or compute
  the last row as the rounded total minus the others), so the screen adds up exactly. Same for 주가 + 환율.
- Receipt line (CLAUDE.md: computed ratios show their inputs): "수익률은 세 수익을 더한 {gain}을 산 금액 전체 {cost}로
  나눈 값이에요." where {cost} is the won cost of every purchase, including shares later sold.
- A part that can't be computed is left out and named, as the existing `noFx` line does. Never counted as 0.
- **KOSPI comparison:** stays on price only (the KOSPI series has no dividends), now with sales: each lot's money buys
  KOSPI on its purchase date (as today), and a sale sells the same fraction of that stock's KOSPI units on the sale
  date. [Q4] Mine = (value now + sale money) ÷ all money put in − 1; KOSPI = (KOSPI units still held × today's close +
  money from KOSPI units sold) ÷ the same money − 1. Dividends stay out of both sides. If the existing label could now be read as
  including dividends, propose a one-line note in the PR.
- **미국 종목 수익, 어디서 왔나** (US stocks still held, lots with a rate):
  - per open lot: 주가 = (today's price − purchase price) × remaining qty × lot rate; 환율 = today's price × remaining
    qty × (today's rate − lot rate). They add up exactly to those lots' won gain (unit test, including after sales).
  - Rate line: the lot rate (weighted by remaining dollar cost when there are several: the 평균 line) and today's rate.
  - Holdings without a lot rate are left out, with the Appendix 1 line.
  - The bar shows only when both parts are 0 or more; otherwise just the two signed numbers.
- **기록 내려받기 (엑셀에서 열려요):** one CSV for all portfolios, RFC 4180: fields with a comma, quote or line break
  are quoted, quotes doubled. UTF-8 with BOM, CRLF line ends, header row in Korean (Appendix 1), one row per record
  (샀어요, 팔았어요, 배당 받았어요, and v19.3d's imported 가진 종목). Dates `YYYY-MM-DD`; numbers plain (no thousands
  separators). Stock codes written so Excel keeps leading zeros (e.g. `="005930"`, our own cell, not user text; check
  Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets). User-text cells (portfolio and stock names) that start with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab
  or CR get a leading `'`. 원화 금액 left blank when there is no rate (never computed at another day's rate). File name
  `baepsae-records-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`. Export only. Check the download on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and PC, under the
  CSP.

## Part C — 관심 종목 (board `Watch2`)

- 종목 tab: a 관심 종목 section at the top when the list isn't empty, opening the full screen (`Watch2`). Company pages

  CLAUDE.md; optional).
- Store (top level, device and account): `watch: { [code]: { at, conds?: [{ k, v, hitAt? }] } }`, at most 30 stocks,
  at most one condition per kind:
  - `pxBelow`: price below v (Korea 원, whole number; US dollars, 2 decimals).
  - `pbrBelow`: PBR below v (1 decimal). A PBR of 0 or less counts as blank.
  - `dyAtLeast`: 배당수익률 at least v % (1 decimal). Korea uses only verified today's-price yields (`dyt`, PR #50; hidden
    for Korean stocks until it merges); US uses the same current dividend rate as v19.5's 배당수익률 list.
- Each condition is checked on today's data. A blank value is never "met" (the chip shows 정보 없음). Met: the chip
  turns green with a check, as on the board. The first day a condition is seen met on this device, store `hitAt` =
  that `pxDate` and show a 관심 종목 조건 card in 오늘 볼 것 for that day. Clear `hitAt` only when fresh data shows the
  condition not met; blank, stale or failed data never clears it. So a condition that stays met doesn't repeat.
  [Q7] `hitAt` stays on each device and is not synced (two devices would otherwise overwrite each other every morning);

## Part I — Shared data

1. **`macro.json` `fxHist`** (v19.6a): USD/KRW daily closes since 2016-01-01 [Q8] (about 6 KB a year), same shape and cut
   as `idxHist`
   (`{ asOf, rows: [[date, close], …] }`), from the `USDKRW=X` series the pipeline already downloads. The rate for a
   date is the close on that date, or the last close before it. `validate_data.py` requires it and checks its shape and
   that `asOf` matches the run's FX date (Jack's yes). The app treats a missing or old `fxHist` as Part A says. A USD
   record dated before 2016 is saved without a rate and is left out of won totals with the existing `noFx` line.
2. **`data.json` `c1w`** (v19.6e): each stock's close on the last trading day before its market's current week, from
   the same series as `price` (Korea official closes; US the same Yahoo closes `price` uses). Meta `wkFrom` and
   `usWkFrom` (those dates). Blank when missing. [Q9] Korea's official closes aren't split-adjusted, so the pipeline
   keeps last week's listed share count with `c1w` and blanks `c1w` when the count changed by more than 20% (splits,
   무상증자, big issues); log the count. US closes come from the same adjusted series as `price`, so splits are already
   handled. Never a partial bar.

All new fields: no NaN or Infinity (`write_json`), dates in KST except US market dates, loud logs with counts.
