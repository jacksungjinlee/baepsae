# Task v19.1 — Company pages in 2.0 (Korea and US), company logos, topic colours

Status: TODO

Designed with Jack in claude.ai chat, 2026-11-05. Boards: `docs/design/v19.1/reference/` (see its README). The boards use
made-up companies and numbers; Korean text on them is proposed copy, not approved copy, unless this file says so.

## Why

Opening a company from 2.0 drops people into the old app's 기업분석, which looks different and which several users found
hard to read. US stocks have no company page at all. This task gives every Korean and US stock a 2.0 company page built
in three layers, so a new investor reads one headline per screen and an experienced one can still dig in.

## Jack's decisions (2026-11-05)

1. Three layers: **요약** (four tiles) → **tabs** (실적 · 가치 · 배당 · 공시, one headline + one chart each) → **자세히**
   (every ratio, its calculation, the industry map, the calculator).
2. Company pages for **Korean and US** stocks. This replaces the CLAUDE.md rule "기업분석 is Korean-listed companies only".
3. **Real company logos are a must.** Test providers first (Part L); mixing several providers is fine.
4. **Topic colours** for icons are approved (Part H). This changes the CLAUDE.md icon rule.
5. Keep **"지금 가격이 가정하는 것"** on the 가치 tab.
6. **내 메모** ships in the first release.
7. US money display: per-share figures in **$ first** (원 small); anything about the user's own money in **원 first** ($ small).
8. The screener and the scatter maps are not dropped: the screener becomes 조건으로 찾기 (v19.1c), the ROE × PBR map moves
   into 가치 자세히 (industry peers only), the treemap and industry ranking go to the 시장 tab later.
9. Build order is the design chat's call (below).

## Part A — Route and shell

- Route `#stocks/<code>` (KR 6-digit code or US ticker) opens the company page inside 2.0. Back returns to where the
  user came from. Every place that now calls `OLD_LINKS.corp` opens the 2.0 page instead. The old app's 기업분석 stays
  reachable through 예전 뱁새 보기.
- Tabs inside the page: 요약 · 실적 · 가치 · 배당 · 공시 (sticky under the header). Route `#stocks/<code>/<tab>` is fine.
- PC (≥1024 px): same content; 요약 and the active tab may sit side by side as on board PcCompany, or one column of
  max 720 px. Phone first.
- Unknown code: the existing "이 종목의 데이터를 찾지 못했어요." pattern, never an empty page.

## Part B — Layer 1: 요약 (board CoSummary)

Top to bottom:

1. **Header:** logo (Part G), name, market · industry (`indName`), price, today's change, content date
   ("11월 3일 종가" / "미국 11월 3일 종가"). US price in $ with 원 small under it (Jack's decision 7).
   - The board shows a 1-year line chart. **Per-stock price history is not in the data**, so v19.1a shows the 52-week
     range bar (`lo`, `hi`) instead. The chart comes with a later pipeline task.
2. **왜 움직였나 line:** reuse 2.0's existing big-move logic (|d1| ≥ 5%, industry moved together) for any stock, held
   or not. Hidden on ordinary days.
3. **Four tiles** (2 × 2), each: topic icon chip, label, a short phrase, one number. Tapping opens the tab. The phrase
   is chosen by a fixed rule (below), never by free text, so words and numbers can't drift apart.
4. **Warning line:** none → "재무 경고 신호는 없어요" + "적자, 빚 부담, 자본잠식 같은 신호를 사업보고서로 확인해요."; any →
   amber line with the count and the first flag, tapping opens the list (reuse the old app's flag rules: 유동비율,
   이자보상배율, 3년 연속 순손실, 자본잠식; US: 부채비율 over 200% only until v19.2).
5. **내 기준과 맞는 정도:** the criteria cards the user picked in 포트폴리오 만들기 (if none, the first two of the 8),
   each with its rank inside the market ("상위 22%"; bottom half: "하위 26%"). Link "기준 카드 8개 모두 보기" opens a sheet
   with all 8 rows and a 시장 전체 / 업종 안에서 toggle (board CoPage, 내 기준 카드로 보면).
6. **Holding line** (only if held): portfolio name, weight, rule status (reuse 2.0 daily-check wording).
7. **내 메모** (Part E).

**Tile rules** (KR and US unless noted; show "판단할 자료가 없어요" when the input is missing):

| Tile | Phrase rule | Number |
|---|---|---|
| 실적 (KR) | `op` rose in both of the last two years → 늘고 있어요; fell in both → 줄고 있어요; latest `op` < 0 → 적자예요; else 들쭉날쭉해요 | 영업이익 2년 새 ±x% (if the base year is ≤ 0, show 영업이익 {latest}억원) |
| 실적 (US, until v19.2) | `g3` ≥ 3 → 매출이 늘고 있어요; ≤ −3 → 매출이 줄고 있어요; else 매출이 비슷해요 | 매출 증가 연 x% (3년) |
| 가치 | PBR ≤ 0.8 × industry median → 업종보다 싼 편이에요; ≥ 1.25 × → 업종보다 비싼 편이에요; else 업종과 비슷해요. Fewer than 5 peers with PBR → 업종 비교 자료가 부족해요 | PBR x배 · 업종 y배 |
| 배당 (KR) | `dps` 0 → 배당이 없어요; latest DPS < previous → 배당을 줄였어요; N ≥ 2 straight increases → N년 연속 늘렸어요; else 비슷하게 줬어요 | 배당수익률 x% |
| 배당 (US, until v19.2) | `dy` 0 or missing → 배당이 없어요; else 배당을 줘요 | 배당수익률 x% |
| 출렁임 | β > 1.15 → 시장보다 큰 편이에요; β < 0.85 → 시장보다 작은 편이에요; else 시장과 비슷해요 (the old app's thresholds) | 베타 x |

"업종" everywhere in 2.0 means the `ind` group (57 groups) inside the same market, as 왜 움직였나 already uses.

## Part C — Layer 2: tabs

Each tab: one headline sentence (the tile phrase or a fixed variant), one chart, two or three numbers, then a link to
자세히 where one exists.

- **실적 (board CoEarn).** Segmented 매출 / 영업이익 / 순이익; big latest number with change vs the year before; three
  annual bars; "업종보다 빨리/느리게 컸어요" comparing `g3` with the industry median `g3`; **주가는 무엇으로 움직였나**
  (reuse the old DecompCard maths: 3년 주가 = 이익 × 눈높이). Rewrite its verdicts as facts only — drop "이런 상승이
  오래가요" and "기대는 식으면 되돌아가요". Proposed headlines: 지난 3년 상승은 대부분 이익 덕분이에요 · 지난 3년 상승은
  이익보다 기대가 만든 몫이 커요 · 주가가 내린 만큼 이익도 줄었어요 · 이익보다 기대가 더 크게 꺾였어요.
  US until v19.2: show `g3`, `opm`, ROE as tiles and one line that yearly figures come later; no bars.
- **가치 (board CoValue).** Headline from the 가치 tile; industry PBR strip (peers as dots, industry median line, this
  company highlighted); PBR and PER tiles with industry medians; **지금 가격이 가정하는 것** card: the implied yearly
  profit growth from the old DcfCard reverse calculation, drawn as two bars next to the company's actual growth (`g3`),
  with "예측이 아니라 지금 가격을 거꾸로 읽은 숫자예요" and the discount rate used. Show it only when the old card's
  `dcfReady` holds and the result sits inside the old card's valid range; otherwise use its two existing out-of-range
  messages. US: hidden until v19.2. Then the 출렁임 line (β with its estimation-error band, as CLAUDE.md requires — use
  the old portfolio tool's band method; if single stocks have no band yet, tell Jack before shipping). Link to 가치 자세히.
- **배당 (board CoDiv).** Headline from the 배당 tile; dividend per share, big; three annual bars (`dps3`); **"100만원어치
  가지고 있으면 1년에 약 x원"** = 1,000,000 × `dy` / 100, rounded to the nearest 1,000원, marked 세전 and "지난 1년 기준";
  배당수익률 and 배당성향 tiles. US: per-share in $, yearly bars after v19.2.
- **공시 (board CoDisc).** v19.1a uses data that already exists: this company's rows from `disc.json` (임원·주요주주 매매,
  국민연금 지분 변동) with counts and amounts, plus a link to the company's filings on DART. The plain-language filing
  list on the board (fixed title templates per filing type, never AI rewording) needs a new daily DART list call in the
  pipeline: later task. US: hidden until v19.2.

## Part D — Layer 3: 자세히

- **가치 자세히 (board CoValueMore):** every ratio (PBR, PER, EV/EBITDA, P/영업현금흐름) with industry median, a one-line
  meaning, an ⓘ that opens the 용어 사전 entry, and the calculation ("시가총액 4,830억 ÷ 자본 총계 4,390억 = 1.1배");
  the **업종 지도** (ROE × PBR, this industry's companies only, dashed industry trend line, this company highlighted);
  the **calculator** (할인율, 5년 뒤 성장률 → implied growth), with 이 가정 저장하기 as in the old card. No fair-value price.
- **실적 자세히:** 이익률 and the 3-year table.
- **기준 카드 8개** sheet (Part B item 5).

## Part G — Logos in the app

- Provider(s) and order as chosen after Part L. Hotlink as the terms require; lazy-load; time out after about 3 s.
- **Fallback mark** whenever there is no logo or it fails: a rounded square in one of ~10 fixed colours picked by
  hashing the code, with the first letter in white. Never a grey placeholder, never a broken image, never a layout jump.
- Logos appear wherever a company is listed in 2.0 (company page, 종목 tab, 포트폴리오, 거래대금 상위, 왜 움직였나).
- Log logo failures with counts in the console (no silent failure), and add a line on the 데이터 출처 page naming the
  provider(s) and saying that logo images load from their servers.
