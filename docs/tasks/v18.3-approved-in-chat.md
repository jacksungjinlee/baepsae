## Decisions (Jack, 2026-11-02)

- **Day-1 rounding: option (a).** A deck bought as planned is quiet on day 1: each stock's band edges are widened by the
  gap whole-share rounding left in the plan (Part C), and 내 덱's weight bar shows the edges actually used today.
- **Home-screen icon moves to 2.0 with Part E.** `manifest.webmanifest` starts at `./app.html`; `app.html` carries the
  manifest and touch-icon links from `template.html`; the preview page carries none.
- **Android installs move too (Jack, 2026-11-02).** `manifest.webmanifest` gets `"id": "/baepsae/baepsae.html"`. The
  spec resolves `id` against the start_url's origin (the site root), so this equals the old installs' identity, their
  start_url https://jacksungjinlee.github.io/baepsae/baepsae.html. Chrome then treats it as the same installed app and
  existing Android icons open 2.0. (`"./baepsae.html"` would resolve to …github.io/baepsae.html: a different app.)
- **"뱁새 2.0 보기" in the old app's header (Jack, 2026-11-02, this wording).** A small underlined link next to the
  title in `src/shell.jsx` (the old app keeps its text in its own code, not in `src/copy/v2.js`), opening `./app.html`.
  Shown in the Korean screen only (2.0 is Korean only); on a phone 420px or narrower it steps aside while a mode is open
  (모드 선택 and the home-screen 뒤로 need the room) and shows on the first screen and every other tab.
