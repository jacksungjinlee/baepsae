## Blockers

**1. [B2] Editing an imported portfolio.** Today 가진 종목 넣기 can only make a new portfolio. Its button is "매일 점검 시작하기", and saving a second portfolio with the same id is refused. Reusing it to edit would silently:
- re-price amount rows at today's close, changing their share count and 내 수익;
- drop rows that aren't in today's data, and rows added by name only;
- lose the portfolio's id, its start date and its records (sales, dividends).

The spec needs to say:
- the same portfolio is updated in place, and rows the user didn't touch keep what they stored;
- which button saves it, with its words;
- whether changing a quantity edits the opening row or records a 샀어요/팔았어요. If a user lowers an imported quantity below a sale they recorded, the store refuses it, and there is no line for that.

Parts D and E also don't fit imported portfolios:
- They have no 빼기/mix settings; the finder would crash without them. Name the defaults.
- "없는 업종 = 0% target", "the swap inherits the holding's target" and the swap note's "목표 비중({x}%)" all assume a target. Imported portfolios have none.

**2. Built portfolios have no daily 한 종목 최대 % or portfolio-rule check.**
- The daily check looks at built portfolios one stock at a time.
- The 한 종목 30% / 한 업종 35% rules run only for imported portfolios.
- 한 종목 최대 % is used only while the plan is made.

So "after saving, the usual daily check reports anything they break" is not true, and there are no 규칙 깨짐 words for it. Fix: either drop those two sentences, or show a neutral preview with the approved "한 종목 최대 {pct}%".

**3. [B1] Drop the planned quantity only; keep the plan price.**
- If any one holding loses its plan price, the whole portfolio falls back to an older calculation:
  - Korea-only: every holding is re-priced at its average purchase price;
  - with any US stock: every gap becomes 0.
- Tested on live data: four holdings at 25% each (삼성전자, 현대차, AAPL, SK하이닉스). Change 삼성전자 25→20 and 현대차 25→30. The two untouched holdings change (SK하이닉스's low edge moves 18.2% → 18.8%). That breaks "every other rule stays as it is".
- With only the planned quantity dropped, your test still works: 25% → 10% while at 25% is flagged the same day.
- Untouched holdings' small gaps still shift a little (AAPL +0.03 → +0.32 pt). The spec should say that's expected.

## B3: does 점검 differ today? Yes.
- **How it works now:** 점검 never gives a stock one combined "상위 x%". It ranks each 기준 separately, and points don't weight it. Its "평균 상위 x%" is one number per 기준, averaged over the holdings by money.
- **Example (11/6 data, 성장 4 · 가치 3 · 수익성 3):** 점검 shows SK하이닉스 as 가치 상위 90%, 성장 3%, 수익성 1%, so it lands in 덜 맞아요. The new single number would say 상위 9%. KB금융 (no 성장 number) would show "—".
- **Suggestion:** keep 점검 as it is (it is the approved rule). Use the single number only where one number per stock is shown: the finder, compare, the 종목 바꾸기 rows and 홈's 내 기준 list.
