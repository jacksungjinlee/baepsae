How this works
  • Pre-pick. Each tile shows the logo most likely to be right, chosen in this order: (1) a logo both services agree on (a Brandfetch and a logo.dev image that look the same); (2) Brandfetch by exact ID: ISIN, then ticker; (3) logo.dev: ticker, then the company's own homepage as filed with DART; (4) Brandfetch by that DART homepage (added because it was right for Kumho Petrochemical); (5) our own website icon (collected from the company's homepage); otherwise the coloured letter mark. An own icon that looked photo-like or hard to see when collected is never pre-picked: it only appears among the alternatives in the Fix panel, as Own icon (check).
  • Exact identifiers only, never names. Ticker: KOSPI and KONEX 005930.KS, KOSDAQ 247540.KQ, US as listed. ISIN from the KRX listing (check digit verified). Domain: the homepage filed with DART, Korea only; platform hosts (blog.naver.com and the like) and a domain two companies share are not used.
  • Agreement is checked only here, in this browser: both images are loaded so the page may read them (CORS), shrunk to a tiny fingerprint and compared. If a provider does not let the page read its images, the agreement rule is skipped for it and the order decides. Not checked yet.
  • Review. Tap a tile to mark it wrong (tap again to undo). Hover (PC) or press and hold (phone) to see it bigger. Judge a grid once the bar at the bottom says it is ready: until then a tile with a dashed border can still change. Then tap Confirm this grid: every tile you did not mark keeps its pre-pick; for each tile you marked, choose among the logos that loaded or the letter mark. A grid counts as reviewed only after Confirm. Companies you have not reviewed keep the letter mark in the app.
  • Badges: agree both services show this logo; low-res the picture the provider sent is smaller than 64 px (a provider may also enlarge a small logo, which this cannot see); saved your confirmed choice; not checked a provider was stopped or timed out for this company.
  • No logo = image error. Every request asks for an error when there is no logo (Brandfetch fallback/404, logo.dev fallback=404), so a miss never shows a placeholder. logo.dev answers a domain it has never seen with a placeholder (HTTP 202) on the first request, so every logo.dev domain hit is loaded again after 5½ minutes and only the second answer counts. That is why a grid can take a few minutes; the next grid loads while you review this one.
  • Checks: before loading, each provider must show a known logo and must give no image for made-up identifiers; while loading, a known logo is checked every 100 loads and a made-up ticker every 300. If a known logo stops loading (rate limit, block, network), the provider pauses and its recent misses are tried again; after three failed checks it stops and Confirm waits until you tap Retry. A route that answers made-up identifiers with an image is switched off.
  • Pace: at most 2 requests a second to Brandfetch and 4 a second to logo.dev, one at a time per route; only the grid you are on and the next unreviewed one load, never all companies at once. Keep this tab in front while it loads.
  • What is saved: only your choices (the source and the exact logo address, or the letter mark), in this browser's storage for this page. Logos are never copied: the app will load them from the provider, as here. Download choices gives a JSON file of the reviewed companies; Load choices reads one back (on another device too). When the same company is in both, the later review date wins (the file wins a tie).
  • Who is here: every Korean company (common shares; preferred shares use their common share's logo; ETFs and funds left out) and every US company in the data shown below, largest market cap first, in grids of 100.
