### Choices I made
- **The band (my decision after review round 1).** Both reviewers found that the foreign bands blank correct numbers on ordinary US companies. NVDA's PBR is 1.39× off because ROE uses average equity. APH's forward yield is 1.19× its trailing dividends after a raise. Up-C companies like IBKR fail all three. In the 11-24 file, 41 of 581 comparable US companies (SNDK aside) are beyond 1.35× on PBR ↔ PER × ROE; 22 are beyond 1.5× and 5 beyond 2.83×. So the band is now max(1.5, √ratio), the same as #176's gate row (`validate_data.us_split_band`, used directly), for PER, PBR and the yield. If a stock's split ratio can't be read, it gets 1.5× (narrowest). Two splits in the window use the smaller one (narrower). **Still possible false positives, all failing safe (blank plus a true line):** a split company whose forward yield is at least 1.5× its trailing dividends; an Up-C structure; a company 1.5× or more off on PBR ↔ PER × ROE (about 4% of US companies by the count above); a company with no PER or ROE (its PBR is blanked as "can't compare", then held after the 30 days until it can be compared and agrees; 80 of 637 US PBRs have no PER or ROE, per Fable). **The cost:** a split smaller than 1.5:1 leaves its error inside the band; a 3:2 split's full 1.5× error stays (the test is "more than"); a halved PBR after a 2:1 split on an NVDA-like company reads 1.44× and stays; an 8:1 split's error between 1.5× and 2.83× stays.
